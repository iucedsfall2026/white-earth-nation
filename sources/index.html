    Geography

    Community indicators describe all residents of the Census tribal area, not enrolled citizens. Economic and energy indicators describe the overlapping reference counties.

    Uncertainty

    ACS values are survey estimates with 90% margins of error, shown as ± values and shaded bands. Consecutive 5-year estimates share four years of data, so compare non-overlapping periods before calling something a trend.

    What is not shown, and why

    Federal funding totals are withheld. The pipeline searched USAspending.gov by recipient name. Those matches have not been verified, cover many years at once, and include contracts and assistance that may not reach either Nation. Publishing the totals would invite people to quote them as Tribal funding, so they stay in the private research repository until they can be verified.

    No facility-level data. Load, outage, tariff, property condition and household-level information require authorization from each Nation and were not collected.

    No targets or rankings. Targets, owners and priorities belong to each Nation and are left blank on purpose.
