Break-even rehabilitation cost by comparison period

Break-even cost = (assistance per year − upkeep per year) × annuity factor (period, rate). Same formula as the project's pipeline.

What this leaves out
  • Real bids, budgets, eligible program costs and property condition. None were available to this project.
  • Whether the household, location, tenure and quality of housing are actually equivalent between the two options.
  • For scale, the HUD 2025 total development cost ceilings recorded in the pipeline run from $438,741 (1 bedroom) to $644,506 (5 bedrooms). These are regulatory limits, not construction costs.
